# Support

For usage questions, feature requests, and reproducible bugs, use the
[GitHub issue tracker](https://github.com/imkks/Kharcha/issues) and choose the
matching template.

Before opening an issue, check the [README](README.md) and
[documentation index](docs/README.md). Include your app version, Android
version, and steps to reproduce. Do not attach real SMS content, account
numbers, transaction amounts, keystores, passwords, or screenshots that expose
financial details. Replace them with synthetic or fully redacted examples.

For security vulnerabilities, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead of
opening a public issue.
